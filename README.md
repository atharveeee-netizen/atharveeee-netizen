### ⚡ About Me

I build at the intersection of **flight avionics, embedded systems, and physics-driven compute**. My work spans custom autonomous UAVs, edge computer vision, RF front-end sensor fusion, and high-frequency optical/RF wireless networks.

- 🚁 **Avionics & Robotics:** Designing, building, and tuning autonomous multirotors with Pixhawk & ArduPilot stacks.
- 📡 **Optical & RF Systems:** Simulating and modeling laser-based LiFi networks and UAV RF front-ends.
- 🧠 **Edge AI & Embedded:** Deploying computer vision pipelines on resource-constrained compute (Raspberry Pi / microcontrollers).
- ❄️ **Quantum & CMOS:** Exploring Cryogenic CMOS architectures and low-temperature semiconductor electronics.

### 🚀 Featured Engineering Projects

#### 🚁 [f450-face-detection-drone](https://github.com/atharveeee-netizen/f450-face-detection-drone)
> **Autonomous Quadcopter with Onboard Edge Vision**
- Custom-built **F450 quadcopter platform** integrated with **Pixhawk flight controller**, GPS lock, telemetry links, and LiPo power distribution.
- Deployed real-time **onboard OpenCV face detection** running on a companion **Raspberry Pi 3B+** interfaced with the flight computer over MAVLink.
- `Pixhawk` · `ArduPilot` · `Raspberry Pi 3B+` · `OpenCV` · `Python` · `LiPo Systems`

#### 📡 [mine-survey-uav-rf](https://github.com/atharveeee-netizen/mine-survey-uav-rf)
> **Reproducibility Codebase for UAV RF Front-End & Sensor-Fusion Paper**
- Research algorithms and calibration models for an autonomous UAV RF front-end deployed for subterranean/mine boundary surveying.
- Implements sensor-fusion routines combining RF signal metrics with navigational telemetry for harsh multipath environments.
- `Sensor Fusion` · `RF Front-End` · `MATLAB / Python` · `ArduPilot Telemetry` · `LaTeX`

#### 💡 [laser-lifi-network](https://github.com/atharveeee-netizen/laser-lifi-network)
> **Laser-Based Indoor LiFi Network with DMD Beam Steering**
- Simulated and modeled indoor high-bandwidth Free-Space Optical Communication (FSO / LiFi).
- Utilized **COMSOL Multiphysics** for Digital Micromirror Device (DMD) optical beam steering coupled with **OptiSystem** network transmission simulations.
- `COMSOL` · `OptiSystem` · `MATLAB` · `Optical Comms` · `Beam Steering`
